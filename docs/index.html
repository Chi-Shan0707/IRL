The three questions

What can be inverted?Demonstrations determine a reward only up to a large equivalence class. What is the honest target?
How do we choose?Maximum margin and maximum entropy are two selection principles — and they make different assumptions.
What must be approximated?Infinite horizons, learned features and unknown dynamics each force an approximation. Which one, and at what cost?

Roadmap

  1. Forward reinforcement learningMDPs, policy evaluation as a linear solve, optimality as a fixed point: why the forward direction is easy.
  2. The inverse problem is ill-posedThe solution set is a polyhedral cone; potential shaping; choosing a reward class.
  3. Selection principlesMaximum margin (Abbeel & Ng) and maximum entropy (Ziebart et al.): the partition function, a worked example, and maximum causal entropy.
  4. Three exits from the tabular worldExit A: infinite and continuous problems. Exit B: learned features. Exit C: unknown dynamics.
  5. Occupancy measures, GAIL and AIRLThe right change of variables, imitation as density-ratio estimation, and recovering rewards that transfer.
  6. Putting it togetherEvery method on two coordinates, a checklist for reading IRL papers, and common errors.
